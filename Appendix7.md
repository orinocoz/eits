Structural notes:

- The ten core BSI module groups (ISMS through IND) have one-to-one structural correspondence with E-ITS at the top level. Individual measure numbers and scope differ due to Estonian adaptation.
- SYS.EE covers X-tee data exchange services and eID components specific to Estonian digital infrastructure and is included in the SYS totals.
- APP.EE covers Estonian-specific applications and AI systems and is included in the APP totals.
- This table is an analytical module-level orientation, not a formal control equivalence mapping.
